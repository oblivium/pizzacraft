Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If iCloud is turned on for PizzaCraft, your recipes and bake journal (including photos) are also kept in your own private iCloud account, so they appear on your other iPhone and iPad. Timers and bake plans stay on the device where you made them.

Nothing is ever sent to me. See the Privacy Policy for details.

My recipes don't show up on my other device.

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is on for PizzaCraft: Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Open PizzaCraft on both and give it a minute. The app's own Settings (the gear on the Calculator) shows whether iCloud is available.

Where are kitchen temperature, manual yeast and poolish?

They're advanced options. Tap the gear at the top of the Calculator and turn on Advanced options. Nothing you've set is lost while they're hidden.

How do bake plans work?

On the Calculator, tap Plan for a Time and pick when you want to eat. PizzaCraft works out when to mix, ball, refrigerate, take the dough out and preheat, and sends a notification at each step. After pizza time you can log the bake in your journal with one tap.

No reminders? Allow notifications for PizzaCraft in Settings › Notifications.

The Home Screen widget isn't showing my timer.

Add the PizzaCraft widget from the widget gallery (touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, then search for PizzaCraft), and make sure a fermentation timer is running in the app.

How do I switch between grams and ounces?

Tap the g / oz button at the top of the Calculator, or choose your units in Settings. It applies everywhere weights are shown.

Can I save my own recipe?

Yes. Tap + on the Recipes tab to write one from scratch, or dial in a formula on the Calculator and tap Save as Recipe.
